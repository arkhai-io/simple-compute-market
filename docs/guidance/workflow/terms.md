# Where terms live

| Terms of | Home |
|---|---|
| one subsystem | that capability's `openspec/specs/<capability>/spec.md`, listed in the repository's capability index |
| the change workflow | `change-workflow`'s specification, its "Terms" |
| the repository across its subsystems | the home the repository's root guidance names |

A subsystem's definition wins inside that subsystem.

A term new to the reader that stays is added to its home in the same change that
introduces it, not left to a later one.

When the owner asks what a mechanism is for or what a word means, answer in
established terms, then correct the description that prompted the question, or
propose the term's entry in its home.
