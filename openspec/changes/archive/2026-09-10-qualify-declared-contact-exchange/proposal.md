# Qualify the local declared contact exchange

Issue: https://github.com/arkhai-io/simple-compute-market/issues/225

## Why

Publication and reviewed delivery have separate installed-wheel tests. One local journey must prove their join without replacing publication with seeded listings or interpreting declaration facts as physical authority.

## What changes

Add one repository-owned integration test using the installed `publish-declarations` command, six packaged TEST declarations, a real signed loopback registry, installed buyer negotiation/introduction transports, durable SQLite and fake SMTP. Retain exact advertised expectations before acceptance; restart after durable capture/completion, mutate the listing and observe independent recipient outcomes without rewriting frozen content. Reuse focused compatibility, physical-path, schema/vector and delivery tests rather than duplicate them.

This is local source qualification only. No release, physical supply, hosted purchase CLI, generic buyer contact-acceptance support, real registry or real email is qualified.

## Permanent documentation impact

The independently reviewed behavior is promoted to the three owning subsystem spec/architecture pairs (`storefront-publication`, `contact-exchange-settlement`, `introduction-delivery`), `docs/development/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/development/DEPLOYMENT_AND_CONFIG.md`, `docs/development/ROADMAP.md`, and capability/campaign indexes. A bounded clarification is appended to `two-sided-contact-delivery` without changing completed tasks or its frozen contract. Parent validation covers the applied permanent documentation and local qualification evidence.
