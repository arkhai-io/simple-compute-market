## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Local declared exchange qualification uses actual publication

The repository SHALL provide a deterministic installed-wheel qualification journey from general declaration publication through signed discovery, exact rateless acceptance, explicit reviewed finalization and two recipient copies. Publication SHALL use the operator command with more than five TEST declarations, not seeded registry/listing rows. Validation SHALL retain advertised option, declaration, machine and requested-term expectations independently of the accepted reply. Local evidence SHALL NOT claim physical supply, generic buyer contact acceptance, release or live delivery qualification.

#### Scenario: A declared exchange survives restart and recipient refusal

- **WHEN** six TEST declarations are published to an owned signed loopback registry and one exact option is accepted and explicitly finalized
- **THEN** acceptance and review create no mail, and finalization durably captures the reviewed contacts/routes and accepted context
- **AND** restart and mutable listing changes preserve the same capture and settlement identity
- **AND** one temporary recipient refusal does not resend the other recipient's accepted copy; both copies contain only counterparty shareable text and the same frozen context

#### Scenario: A self-consistent reply substitutes the retained machine

- **WHEN** a reply replaces machine facts and recomputes its internal context digest
- **THEN** the qualification caller refuses it against the independently retained selection, even if internal provenance validation succeeds
