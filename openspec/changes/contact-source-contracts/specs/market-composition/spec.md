## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Declaration source meaning remains domain-owned

Bare-metal declaration identifiers SHALL name public seller assertions, not machine registrations or physical inventory. Core SHALL retain its schema-opaque unbacked binding with null site, pool and Physical Resource authority. Domain admission SHALL validate declaration projection and immutable publication intent; the contact kit SHALL consume only explicit eligibility and a domain-supplied accepted-context digest, without importing bare-metal types. Discovery SHALL remain `vms.compute` and negotiation SHALL remain `bare_metal.v1`.

#### Scenario: An unbacked declaration is accepted

- **WHEN** bare metal accepts a context-eligible contact option
- **THEN** the accepted plan binds the domain capture without a physical placeholder or an upward dependency from the contact kit
